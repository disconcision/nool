Where it came from

Back in 2022, I had a prototype of a game aimed at making math more tangible, a long-running obsession of mine. It had a lot going for it, but the fundamental mechanic, based on selecting transformations from a menu, lacked the juicy tactility I craved. I eventually came up with a primitive version of what would become model-driven dragging, but implementing it seemed daunting, so it sat on my whiteboard for the next two years, haunting me every time I went to make a sandwich. — from Declarative Dragging
